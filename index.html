Inside the system

Built around the fundamentals.

Safe chemistry, practical power electronics and flexible communication — configured for the way your system needs to work.

01 / CHEMISTRY

LiFePO4 platform

Long-cycle lithium iron phosphate storage, with ternary lithium available for selected portable and custom systems.

02 / POWER

Pure sine wave output

Portable systems and the integrated ESS use pure sine wave inverters for clean, stable AC power.

03 / SOLAR

MPPT charging

Solar-ready controllers match voltage and input conditions across portable and all-in-one systems.

04 / CONTROL

Open communication

CAN, RS485, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi options support system integration and remote visibility.
