Before you write

Common questions.

Can you customize voltage, housing and communication?

OEM / ODM customization is available for selected systems. Housing, connector, communication protocol, labels and packaging can be discussed against the project requirements.

Are batteries suitable for both solar and backup?

The storage and all-in-one systems accept solar input when paired or integrated with the appropriate inverter and MPPT controller. Final compatibility should be confirmed for the complete system.

What shipping documentation is available?

The reference product range lists CE, CB, CCC, FC, MSDS and UN38.3. Export documents can include UN38.3 reports, MSDS, packing lists and commercial invoices, subject to the final product and destination.

Can you help select capacity?

Yes. Share the equipment to be powered, required runtime, available solar input and installation constraints. We will help match the system.
