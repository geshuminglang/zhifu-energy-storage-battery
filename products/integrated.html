System flow

Solar, grid and battery — coordinated.

The integrated ESS accepts solar and AC input, manages the battery, and delivers pure sine wave output to priority loads.

Generate

PV arrays feed the integrated MPPT input across the supported voltage window.

Store

LiFePO4 battery modules hold 5 kWh or 8 kWh nominal energy, with 90% usable capacity.

Manage

AC and PV charging are coordinated through the integrated control platform.

Deliver

A pure sine wave inverter supplies connected and priority loads at 3.6 kW or 5 kW.
