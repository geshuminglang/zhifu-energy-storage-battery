Who we are

One team from cell assembly to export support.

Situated in the urban manufacturing hub of Huizhou, Guangdong, ZHIFU Technology designs and manufactures lithium battery systems for energy storage and global product programmes.

Our manufacturing and R&D experience spans high-performance lithium-ion batteries, matched-cell assembly, battery management, testing and product configuration. We support standard systems and work directly with customers on OEM / ODM requirements.
